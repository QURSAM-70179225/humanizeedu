        Frequently asked questions

        Is HumanizeEdu AI free?

        Yes. It is completely free and needs no sign-up or login. Each visitor can run up to 30 checks per hour.

        How does the AI-generated percentage work?

        It is an estimate based on writing patterns that AI detectors tend to flag: overused AI words such as delve and tapestry, very even sentence lengths, and stock transitions like furthermore and moreover. It is not a Turnitin, GPTZero or Copyleaks result.

        Is the plagiarism score a real plagiarism check?

        No. It is a plagiarism-risk estimate. It flags quotes without citations, facts and definitions without a source, and generic stock phrasing. It does not compare your text against the web or other papers.

        Will humanizing change my argument or citations?

        No. Your thesis, facts and numbers stay the same, and direct quotes and citations such as (Smith, 2021) are locked in place.

        Is my text stored?

        No. There are no accounts and no database. Your text is processed to produce the results and then discarded.

        Can I use it for my assignments?

        Use it to polish writing you wrote yourself and to fix false-positive AI flags. Always cite your sources and follow your institution's policy on AI tools.

        Want to learn more? Read our free guides on avoiding false AI-detection flags, APA citations and paraphrasing without plagiarism.

        Built for honesty, not ghostwriting

        HumanizeEdu is for polishing work you wrote yourself, fixing false-positive AI flags, and making sure every source gets credit. It never invents facts or sources, and nothing you paste is stored.
