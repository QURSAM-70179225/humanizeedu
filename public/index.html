        How it works

        01

        Scan

        We estimate how AI-generated your text reads (from buzzwords, repetitive rhythm and stock transitions) and how much of it looks borrowed (from uncited quotes, facts and generic phrasing).

        02

        Humanize

        Groq AI rewrites the text in the tone you pick. It varies sentence length and drops words like "delve" and "tapestry", and your quotes and citations stay exactly as they are.

        03

        Compare

        You see both percentages before and after, the sentences that still need a source, and APA, MLA, Chicago or IEEE citation hints.

        Built for honesty, not ghostwriting

        HumanizeEdu is for polishing work you wrote yourself, fixing false-positive AI flags, and making sure every source gets credit. It never invents facts or sources, and nothing you paste is stored.

        • Your argument, thesis and facts are never changed.
        • Direct quotes and citations are locked in place.
        • Uncited quotes and facts are flagged so you can credit them.
        • The percentages are estimates, not Turnitin or GPTZero results.
        • Always follow your institution's policy on AI tools.
