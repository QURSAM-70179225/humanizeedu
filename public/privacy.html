Privacy

HumanizeEdu AI has no accounts and no database.

  • Your text isn't stored. It's processed to produce your results, then discarded. We keep no history of what you paste.
  • AI rewriting: to produce the rewrite, your text is sent to an AI provider (Groq, or Google Gemini as a backup). Their own privacy terms apply.
  • No tracking cookies. To prevent abuse, we keep a short-lived count of requests per network address in memory, and it resets every hour.

Don't paste personal or confidential information.
