Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

HumanizeEdu AI ("we", "us") is a free writing tool. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

1. Text you submit

  • There are no user accounts and no database. We do not store the text you paste, and we keep no history of your checks.
  • To produce a rewrite, your text is sent to an AI provider: Groq, or Google Gemini as a backup. Their own privacy terms apply to that processing.
  • Please don't paste personal, sensitive or confidential information.

2. Technical information

Like most websites, our hosting provider (Render) may record standard server logs such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested, for security and reliability. To prevent abuse, we also keep a short-lived count of requests per network address in memory, and it resets every hour.

3. Cookies and advertising

HumanizeEdu AI itself does not use tracking cookies. However, we use (or may use) Google AdSense to show ads that help keep the site free.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this and other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising in Google's My Ad Center, or opt out of some third-party vendors' cookies at aboutads.info.
  • Learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are shown a consent message, so you can choose how cookies are used before personalized ads are shown.

4. Other services

We load fonts from Google Fonts. If you click a "Support" link, you'll go to a third-party donation site (such as Buy Me a Coffee or Ko-fi), and its privacy policy applies there.

5. Children

This site is intended for students aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Your choices

Because we don't store your text or create accounts, there is usually no personal data for us to delete. You can control cookies through your browser settings and the advertising opt-out links above.

7. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date above shows the latest version.
