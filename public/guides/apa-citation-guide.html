APA 7 citation quick guide for students

By Qursam Bibi · 7 min read · Updated November 2026

APA style (7th edition) is used across psychology, education, engineering, business and the social sciences. This cheat sheet covers what students need most often. Bookmark it.

In-text citations: the basics

Every time you use someone else's idea, data or words, add an in-text citation with the author's last name and the year. There are two ways to write it:

TypeParentheticalNarrative
One author(Khan, 2021)Khan (2021) found that…
Two authors(Khan & Ali, 2021)Khan and Ali (2021) argue…
Three or more authors(Khan et al., 2021)Khan et al. (2021) showed…
Organization as author(World Health Organization [WHO], 2020), then (WHO, 2020)The World Health Organization (WHO, 2020)…
No date(Khan, n.d.)Khan (n.d.) explains…

In APA 7, use "et al." for three or more authors starting from the first citation. Use "&" inside parentheses and "and" in running text.

Direct quotes

When you copy exact words, put them in quotation marks and add the page number:

  • One page: (Khan, 2021, p. 42)
  • Page range: (Khan, 2021, pp. 42–43)
  • No page numbers (e.g. a website): use a paragraph number, (Khan, 2021, para. 3)

Block quotes: a quote of 40 words or more goes on a new line, indented 0.5 inch, without quotation marks, with the citation after the final punctuation.

Quote only when the exact wording matters. Most of the time, paraphrasing shows better understanding (and you still cite it). See how to paraphrase without plagiarism.

The reference list

Every in-text citation needs a matching entry in the reference list at the end, and every reference must be cited in the text.

  • Title the page References, centered and bold.
  • Put entries in alphabetical order by the first author's last name.
  • Use a hanging indent (first line flush left, the following lines indented 0.5 inch) and double spacing.
  • Use initials for first names: Khan, A. R.

Journal article

Khan, A. R., & Ali, S. (2021). Cloud adoption in Pakistani universities. Journal of Educational Technology, 14(2), 115–130. https://doi.org/10.xxxx/xxxxx

The journal name and volume number are in italics. The issue number in parentheses is not.

Book

Ahmed, F. (2019). Introduction to database systems (2nd ed.). Example Publishing.

The book title is in italics, in sentence case, meaning only the first word, proper nouns and the word after a colon are capitalized.

Web page

World Health Organization. (2020, March 3). Title of the web page in sentence case. https://www.example.org/page

If the author and the website name are the same, leave the site name out.

Chapter in an edited book

Malik, H. (2018). Title of the chapter. In R. Shah (Ed.), Title of the book (pp. 45–67). Example Publishing.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Citing a fact or statistic with no source at all, which is the most common cause of plagiarism flags.
  • Putting a quote in your essay without page numbers.
  • An in-text citation with no matching reference, or the other way round.
  • Using "et al." in the reference list (APA lists up to 20 authors there).
  • Writing the full first name instead of initials in references.

APA vs. MLA vs. IEEE at a glance

StyleIn-text exampleCommon in
APA 7(Khan, 2021, p. 42)Social sciences, education, business
MLA 9(Khan 42)Literature, languages, humanities
IEEE[1]Engineering, computer science, IT

Always check your course outline or instructor's instructions, since some departments have their own variations.
