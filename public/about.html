About HumanizeEdu AI

HumanizeEdu AI is a free writing tool for students. It helps you polish writing you did yourself, see which parts read as robotic or borrowed, and fix missing citations before you submit.

Why I built it

I'm Qursam Bibi, an Information Engineering Technology student at The University of Lahore. Around me, students were getting their own essays flagged as "AI-generated", often because they wrote in careful, formal English, or because English is their second language. Most of the tools online either promise to "beat" detectors, or charge for basic feedback.

I wanted something different: a free tool that explains why a piece of writing looks robotic or borrowed, helps you improve it in your own voice, and pushes you to cite your sources properly.

What it does

  • AI-generated estimate: checks for patterns detectors often flag, such as overused AI vocabulary, very even sentence rhythm, and stock transitions.
  • Plagiarism-risk estimate: highlights quotes without citations, facts and definitions without a source, and generic phrasing.
  • Natural rewrite: uses AI (Groq's Llama models) to vary sentence rhythm and remove filler, while keeping your argument, facts, quotes and citations unchanged.
  • Free guides on citing in APA, paraphrasing properly and handling false AI flags.

Our principles

  • Honesty first. The tool is for improving your own work, not for submitting work you didn't write. We never invent facts or sources.
  • Clear about limits. Our percentages are estimates based on writing patterns. They are not Turnitin, GPTZero or Copyleaks results, and the plagiarism figure doesn't search the web or other papers.
  • Private by design. There are no accounts and no database, and the text you paste isn't stored. See our Privacy Policy.
  • Free. No sign-up and no paywall. The site may show ads to cover running costs.
